Welcome to CDI Counselling Service

CDI Counselling offers a safe, confidential space for individuals, couples, and families to address challenges like depression, anxiety, and relationship issues. Our services are affordable, with free sessions available for those eligible.

Our Services

We provide high-quality, low-cost counselling services to make therapy accessible to more people. Free sessions are available for those unemployed, subject to status.
